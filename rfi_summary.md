## 📊 Executive Summary: RFI Insights from Roadside Construction Projects

Between **November 2024 and April 2025**, a total of **1500 RFIs** were submitted across major roadside infrastructure projects such as *Lake Scott Bypass Project*, *New John Bypass Project*, and *East Steven Bypass Project*.

### 🚧 Key Findings

- **Top RFI Topics:**  
  The most frequently raised issues involved **Utility Relocation**, **Stormwater Drainage**, and **Lane Closures**, representing a significant portion of all RFIs.

- **Average Time to Close:**  
  RFIs took an average of **16.5 days** to resolve. RFIs submitted to **Civil Engineer** tended to have longer response times.

- **Delays:**  
  Approximately **52.8%** of RFIs were associated with project delays.  
  Notably, **High-priority RFIs** were **3.3x more likely** to cause delays than Low-priority ones.

- **Cost Impact:**  
  High-priority RFIs had a median cost impact of **$128,458**, compared to **$28,771** for Medium and **$5,629** for Low-priority RFIs.

- **Project Phase Risk:**  
  The **Inspection** and **Paving** phases showed the highest rate of delay-inducing RFIs, indicating a need for more proactive coordination during these stages.
